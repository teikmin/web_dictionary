One-click contribution flow for OpenDesign (nexu-io/open-design) — even for non-coders. Pick one of four cards (ship a Skill or Design System you made with OD; translate docs; fix a typo / write a blog; report a bug), the agent validates and opens a PR (or issue) for you. Trigger words contribute to open design, ship my OD skill, ship my OD design system, translate OD docs, report an OD bug, od-contribute.
